# GHG example activity data (fictional)

`activities.csv` describes an **invented** company ("Example Manufacturing
Co.", the same fictional company as the materiality example). Quantities,
sites and the market-based factor are made up. Only the emission factors
applied to them are real (UK Government GHG Conversion Factors 2025).

Row A9 deliberately has no market-based factor, to show that the
market-based Scope 2 total is reported as incomplete rather than guessed.

`trips.csv` (business travel, Scope 3 category 6) and `commuting_survey.csv`
(employee commuting, category 7) are **invented** in the same way: trip
distances, hotel nights, survey answers and office days are made up; only
the UK Government 2025 Scope 3 factors applied to them are real. The survey
covers 403 of the 438 (fictional) employees; the other 35 walk or cycle
(zero emissions, so no row). The worked example also adds 41,250 FTE
homeworking hours (55 hybrid head-office staff x 100 home days x 7.5 h).
